### Banned words

### Rules
- Use the manufacturer's own typeface for that make's creative (§2). Never substitute another manufacturer's face, even one that looks close. Our older BannerNow templates contain a few stray cross-brand fonts (for example Stratum GMC on Honda's 160×600). Those are mistakes; do not copy them.
- Two weights for DealerCentives-style creative: Regular 400 and Bold 700. Bold is for the figures, the vehicle line, CTAs and headings. Regular is for year/make, labels, terms, units and disclosures. Never 800/900 in that system. The banner system has its own weight scheme (§4.2).
- "Honda" is never written in all capitals, anywhere, including uppercase-styled eyebrows and labels, and even when the feed sends "HONDA". The whole phrase goes to title case ("2026 Honda Pilot Elite", never "2026 HONDA PILOT ELITE" or "Honda SPECIALS").
- Do not uppercase text for style. Headings and labels are title case. Model and trim names keep the manufacturer's own casing (CR-V, RAV4, bZ4X, TrailSport, LYRIQ, i-FORCE MAX). A few luxury banner styles use capitals on purpose (§4.2); those are the only exceptions.
- Print the drivetrain in a vehicle name only when that model is sold both ways (2WD and AWD/4WD). Use the per-make lineup in §6.4. This is a manufacturer compliance requirement.
- Never fabricate a term. If a value is unknown, drop its clause. If a required term is missing, drop the whole offer rather than show partial legal text.
- Every payment claim carries its required terms (lease: due at signing, number and amount of payments, security deposit, mileage/overage or the Reg M sentence; APR: APR spelled out, term, down payment, repayment per $1,000, credit qualifier). See §7.
- Disclosures are verbatim. Never truncated, re-cased, abbreviated or reordered. The layout grows to fit the disclosure, never the reverse.
- One claim, one disclosure. Each scene or slide carries its own disclosure. Never show a figure on screen with another scene's disclosure, or with none.
- Name the lender the offer names. Never infer the captive lender from the make. Never relabel Southeast Toyota Finance as Toyota Financial Services.
- Never show an expired offer. Dates are written MM/DD/YYYY. An offer is valid through 23:59:59 dealer-local time on its end date.
- Some dealer groups' wording is legally approved and must stay byte-for-byte: Luther Dealer Group, MXS QuickGen stores, and any offer with approved copy (§7.9). Do not rewrite, shorten or "improve" it.
- Lease: any advertised lease payment carries all of: that it is a lease; total due at signing; number and amount of payments; security deposit; mileage allowance and per-mile rate as a pair, or the Reg M sentence verbatim: "An extra charge may be imposed at the end of the lease term based on the difference between the residual value of the vehicle and its realized value."
- Lease: every advertised lease names its example vehicle's trim. Never print "$0.00/mile" or "$0 customer down payment". "$0 Due at Signing" only from a real structured 0; "$0 Down Payment" only on dealer-custom offers, never manufacturer offers.
- Finance/APR: carries "Annual Percentage Rate (APR)" spelled out on first use, the term, a down-payment statement, repayment terms ("{N} monthly payments of $X.XX per $1,000 financed") and the credit qualifier; if any cannot be built, drop the APR offer.
- A manufacturer's promotional APR and its rebates are mutually exclusive; the disclaimer must say so.
- Cash: VIN-specific ads name the VIN and carry the one-vehicle sentence; model-wide creative must not show a VIN or stock number. Never advertise savings when the price is above MSRP. Customer-group cash uses the program's own name and is never summed into a figure every shopper sees.
- Every offer carries the fee sentence (tax, title, license, registration and fees); when offers are combined it is said once.
- Eligibility qualifiers (lessee, loyalty, conquest, military, graduate, first responder…) sit directly after the payment, never sentences later.
- Disclosures are verbatim: never truncated, re-cased, abbreviated or reordered. In video, never hide the disclosure behind an end card or a "see details"; extend the scene rather than cut it short.
- Stock renders carry "Vehicle shown may differ".
- Dates are written MM/DD/YYYY ("Offer ends MM/DD/YYYY."). Expired offers are removed everywhere; an expiry date is never fabricated. When several offers share a date, it is said once.
- Everything that is not a model or trim name is title case; model and trim names keep the manufacturer's spelling (CR-V, RAV4, bZ4X, TrailSport, LYRIQ, i-FORCE MAX).
- Name the lender the offer names; never infer the captive lender from the make.

SETF copy requires a security deposit unless waived: "Security deposit required with exception of prior Southeast Toyota Finance (SETF) financing history and/or SETF credit rating in which a security deposit may be waived." Do not assume "$0 security deposit" for SETF.
- Gulf States Toyota (TX/OK/LA/AR/MS): the offer governs.
- No finance-conditional rebates inside an advertised price. Phrases that signal one: "must be financed with", "in lieu of".

- Quoted MSRP phrase: "Total SRP of $X".
- Lessons from the Toyota dealer advertising (TDMC) audit of our widgets:
- terms must be legible, never baked tiny into art;
- the full disclosure is never collapsed;
- match the exact configuration (a "Tacoma 2WD SR5" offer must not appear on a 4WD unit);
- never assume a $0 security deposit;
- never show an expired or cross-unit offer.
